# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project free from harassment and
welcoming to people of all backgrounds and experience levels.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, discuss evidence and technical choices rather than people,
acknowledge uncertainty, protect private data, and help maintain a constructive
scientific community.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, publication of private information, and
other conduct that makes participation unsafe are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

After the public repository is opened, report conduct issues through a private
repository contact channel rather than a public issue. Project maintainers may
remove content or restrict participation when necessary to protect the
community. Enforcement decisions should be documented privately and handled
proportionately.
