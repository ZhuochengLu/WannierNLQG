# Nonorthogonal localized basis inputs

This guide describes localized nonorthogonal H/S/r inputs, their supported response and sampling options, and their numerical limitations. The orthogonal model and its specialized Fourier/response route remain available. Successful execution does not establish finite-model convergence or material accuracy.

Start with [representation and units](#representation-and-units), then see the [response scope](#response-and-public-runtime-scope), [Fourier execution](#uniform-full-bz-fft-and-mixed-execution), [OpenMX input](#explicit-openmx-boundary), and [adaptive integration](#adaptive-ic-and-shg-integration).

## Representation and units

`WannierNLQG.Core.NonorthogonalLocalizedModel` stores lattice **rows** in Angstrom, integer translations as three-row **columns**, H in eV with shape `(N,N,NR)`, S (dimensionless) with the same shape, and position in Angstrom with shape `(N,N,3,NR)`. Every R/-R partner and the home cell are explicit. There are no Wannier degeneracy weights. With fractional k the Fourier phase is `exp(+2pi*i*dot(k,R))`; Cartesian derivatives multiply by `i*lattice'*R`, with no additional factor of 2pi. Orbital/spin ordering is part of input provenance. The position translation identity is

```math
r_R-r_{-R}^{\dagger}=R_{\mathrm{cart}}S_R.
```

The original file and raw H/S/r arrays remain immutable provenance. Ordinary construction explicitly pair-projects effective H/S and r consistently. For a partner pair, effective H/S is the adjoint pair average. With translation t and effective S_R, define q=(raw r_R+raw r_-R')/2; effective r_R=q+t*S_R/2 and effective r_-R=(q-t*S_R/2)'. Every spectrum, derivative and response uses this same effective model. Diagnostics retain raw and effective defects, total and maximum entry adjustments, units and reporting thresholds. Finite defects above these thresholds produce numerical warnings. They do not silently overwrite raw operators or automatically classify an ordinary calculation as diagnostic-only. Missing partners, nonfinite arrays, invalid shapes/units and nonpositive metric remain errors; no metric clipping or band removal is performed.

Reporting tolerances are named `hamiltonian_representation_tolerance_ev` (Frobenius norm per R, eV), `overlap_representation_tolerance` (Frobenius per R, dimensionless) and `position_translation_tolerance_angstrom` (maximum entry per R, Angstrom). Defaults are 1e-10 for programmatic models and 1e-8 at the importer. The convenience `representation_tolerance` assigns these three numeric values, not a shared physical unit. Source/model digests bind raw and effective arrays and the measured adjustment record. Large adjustments must be assessed before interpreting a material result; completed execution does not establish accuracy. `position_r=nothing` explicitly admits H/S-only work. This absence is preserved through digesting and matrix outputs; it never becomes a zero position operator. Position, velocity and response capabilities then fail before execution.

## Spin and Bloch conventions

The model accepts explicit keywords `spin_representation=:spinless|:collinear|:spinor`, `magnetic_state=:nonmagnetic|:collinear|:noncollinear`, `spin_orbit_coupling`, and `spin_ordering=:scalar|:blocked|:interleaved`. Spinful S/r are currently spin identities; spin-dependent metrics or position operators require a future supported contract. Declared collinear H is exactly uncoupled and its two actual generalized spectra are solved independently, then merged into the shared charge-response route with one physical chemical potential. No spinor or collinear state is counted twice. SOC or noncollinear magnetism uses the full spinor; a collinear magnetic moment does not justify discarding mixing. The six requested magnetic/SOC cases are represented by these explicit choices.

`orbital_centers_cartesian` has shape `(3,N)`, in Cartesian Angstrom in persisted state order; spin components repeat the same spatial centers. Convention II remains the default. `NonorthogonalLocalizedInput(...; wannier_center_convention="Convention_I")` requires explicit centers. In Convention I each source term has displacement `R_cart+tau_ket-tau_bra` and position coefficient `r_R-tau_bra*S_R`; all H/S first and second derivatives and r first derivatives use that same phase and displacement **before** applying the original D_eta formula below. No already regularized band connection is transported to force equality. For aligned nondegenerate band frames, eta=0 agrees between conventions. At finite eta the off-diagonal connection difference is `-eta^2/(Delta^2+eta^2)*(Q+Q')/2`, with `Q=C_II'*S_II*diag(tau)*C_II`. These differences must be measured and disclosed with convention, eta, spin/SOC, backend and integration/termination metadata; eta or optical broadening is not changed to hide them. Existing scalar degeneracy/finite-eta limitations remain.

Focused native source-jet, spin admission and OpenMX parsing controls have passed on frozen development snapshots. Collinear SC/IC/SHG charge brackets for both conventions and both orderings agree with the sum of separately evaluated actual blocks at one common chemical potential, and with the equivalent full spinor. These are bounded engineering/numerical controls, not complete spinful scientific qualification. A frozen foundation snapshot has completed bounded independent spin/native-convention source, scalar geometry and charge comparisons, plus single-process SC adaptive and orthogonal regression controls. Those historical results do not transfer to subsequently changed sources. The implementation provides uniform FFT/Mixed, adaptive IC/SHG and the bounded global-budget MPI protocol described below. Implementation, source-bound independent science and MPI engineering acceptance remain separate evidence states. Historical reports qualify only their recorded sources and contracts. Promotion requires explicit user confirmation after final frozen-source qualification.

## Generalized spectrum and scalar matrix elements

The backend solves `H_eff*C=S_eff*C*Diagonal(E)` with a positive metric. Nonpositive or nonfinite S_eff and nonfinite eigensystems fail. It measures relative metric conditioning, normalized generalized residual and `norm(C'*S*C-I)`. Default reporting thresholds are a relative metric floor 1e-10, eigen residual 1e-11 and normalization 1e-10. Finite excesses are numerical warnings in ordinary calculations, not implicit publication gates. Diagnostics independently retain raw/effective residuals and normalization, and C'*(raw S-S_eff)*C. Eigensolver Hermitian wrappers use the effective upper triangle, with the remaining rounding residual recorded. Cached diagnostics bind source, k point, spectrum slot and generation. The scientific comparison adapter imposes its own explicit accuracy contract; ordinary computation readiness does not transfer a scientific PASS.

Let bars mean `C'*X*C` and `B_a=r_a+i*S_a=r_a'`. The scalar metric prescription uses the shared `Numerics(denominator_regularization=eta)` in eV, whose default is 0.001 eV, independently of optical delta broadening. Define `Delta_nm=E_m-E_n`, `G_a=C'*S_a*C` and `N_a,nm=H_a,bar,nm-(E_n+E_m)*G_a,nm/2`. Then

```math
D^{a,eta}_{nm}=-G^a_{nm}/2+N^a_{nm}\frac{\Delta_{nm}}{\Delta_{nm}^2+eta^2},\quad n\ne m;
\qquad D^{a,eta}_{nn}=-G^a_{nn}/2+i\mathrm{Re}\,\bar B^a_{nn},
\qquad A^{a,eta}=iD^{a,eta}+\bar B^a.
```

The diagonal choice is a pointwise parallel gauge; diagonal connection derivatives are not supplied. Each transformed derivative uses the same tangent, `X_;b=D_eta_b'*Xbar+Xbar*D_eta_b+C'*X_b*C`, and differentiates the same regularizer with `f'(Delta)=(eta^2-Delta^2)/(Delta^2+eta^2)^2`. This supplies the non-diagonal covariant position derivative. For eta=0 and nonzero gaps it reaches the isolated-band derivative. An exactly zero interband gap with eta=0 makes this scalar derivative undefined and raises an error. The existing formal metric SC entry retains this eta=0 isolated-band option, with default eta=0.001 eV; orthogonal matrix roles and SHG regularizers still require positive widths. This retained scalar expert interface does not itself select bare-outer optics. SHG separately requests the optional cutoff optical capability described below. A nonzero small gap has no blanket rejection threshold. Positive eta permits an exactly degenerate point under this declared scalar approximation; it does not qualify non-Abelian or block-projector geometry.

Physical velocity remains `V_nm=H_a,bar,nm-E_m*G_a,nm+i*(E_n-E_m)*B_a,bar,nm` in eV Angstrom. The column energy here follows from defining B to include `i*S_a`; equivalently use the row energy E_n together with the original `r_a,bar`. The finite-eta commutator defect is `i*(E_n-E_m)*A_eta_nm-V_nm=-N_nm*eta^2/(Delta_nm^2+eta^2)` off diagonal. It is measured rather than forced to zero.

Finite eta gives approximate transformed derivatives and an approximate energy Hessian; its antisymmetry is reported without silently symmetrizing. At an exact degeneracy, diagonal energy slopes are Rayleigh slopes in the metric-normalized solver frame, and can change under a rotation inside the degenerate block. These are not unique derivatives of sorted scalar bands. The diagnostic record states eta, approximation, frame prescription, minimum gap, Ward/normalization and Hermiticity residuals, commutator defect, regularization suppression and Hessian antisymmetry. Eta and frame sensitivity must be assessed before interpreting a material response. No material convergence follows from a completed calculation.

Expert calls use `MatrixElements.MatrixElementWorkspace` and compile their explicit plan with `source_gauge_required=false`. They share `KPointMatrixData` and its unique `KPointSpectrum` owner with the ordinary route. Metric-specific position storage carries covariant derivatives; the ordinary orthogonal connection derivative routine is not applied to a nonunitary C.

## Response and public Runtime scope

`TaskConfig(model=Core.NonorthogonalLocalizedInput(model), ...)` admits direct Fourier execution for Band KPath; Conventional intrinsic AHC, SC, IC, SHG, linear optical response and linear transport Integral/KSlice; scalar interband quantum metric, interband Berry curvature, full band Berry curvature and internal quantum metric with its derivatives and Christoffel KSlice; and explicitly defined finite-model Conventional orbital magnetization Integral/KSlice. Registry observables, units, occupations, mesh weights, charge/constants and output layouts remain shared with the orthogonal route. Integral SC/IC uses FullTensor; existing TensorComponents remains the JC/THG four-axis selector. IC uses the metric Hellmann-Feynman energy slope, not H_a alone.

Ambient connection curvature is lowered before transformation to the band frame. For beta_a=S^-1*B_a and derivatives indexed (a,b)=partial_b of component a,

```math
F^{lower}_{ab}=S(partial_a beta_b-partial_b beta_a-i[beta_a,beta_b]),
\qquad F^{band}_{ab}=C^\dagger F^{lower}_{ab}C.
```

The beta derivative uses linear solves: `partial_b beta_a = S \ (partial_b B_a-S_b*beta_a)`. Full scalar band Berry curvature adds the ambient diagonal to `-2Im sum_m A_eta[n,m,a]*A_eta[m,n,b]`. Ambient curvature alone is not full band Berry curvature. H/S/r supply this connection curl; they do not supply material orbital or Full metric completion.

SHG requests `OPTICAL_CUTOFF_VERTICES` from the public MatrixElements layer. Its `cutoff_optical_position_vertices` uses bare external r0, the existing metric r_eta for intermediate links, and Gcut=R+I0 Q from a local normalized H/S/r source jet. The full source connection diagonal, overlap derivatives and moving-basis connection are retained inside Gcut; the existing point-parallel zero diagonal must not substitute for that source connection. The retained expert `optical_position_vertices` still returns its original r_eta/G_Deta aliases. See [SecondHarmonicGeneration](../theory/SecondHarmonicGeneration.md) for the normalized source-jet derivation and cutoff prescription.

Geometry `denominator_regularization=0.001` eV controls r_eta and the Gcut commutators. It stays independent of `intermediate_regularization=0.001` eV, which weights three-band energy combinations; optical `broadening=0.025` eV and `low_frequency_broadening=0.025` eV also retain their defaults. Both geometry and intermediate widths must be finite and positive. Nonorthogonal `eta_correction` defaults false and explicit true remains unsupported; the orthogonal default is unchanged. No extra TOML mode, input operator or dependency is needed.

Only the optical vertices/derivative in the first five SHG terms change. The BCD term still selects bands whose existing adjacent gaps exceed the response threshold and uses the separate bare isolated-band curvature with ambient and metric connection contributions. The semiclassical seventh term keeps the existing metric physical velocity and approximate HF Hessian. Bare endpoint I0 is zero strictly below 1e-7 eV and reciprocal at equality and above; finite R and connection terms survive. This is not a complete degenerate-block response, and finite-eta invariance under arbitrary k-dependent basis changes is not asserted. S=I comparisons match both widths and disable eta_correction on both routes; independent parameter settings need not produce identical results.

Finite-model modern orbital response requires `input_semantics=:defined_finite_model` and an actually flat ambient connection. Its metric spectral wrapper retains Aeta, physical velocity and ambient curvature, bypassing orthogonal position reconstruction. Zero external completion is permitted only for this declared complete finite model. Nonflat ambient, unexpected material completion and all orthogonal C*C' projector helpers are rejected. Real-material derivative-overlap, energy-connection and energy-overlap operators remain unavailable.

SC's length-gauge prefactor uses ordered optical fields and complete ordered band pairs, `pi*e_el^3/(2*hbar^2)` with `e_el=-|e|`, eV delta conversion and bulk A/V^2. Independent reports may explicitly convert to microampere/V^2 with a pinned 1e6 factor; public tables retain A/V^2. Spin multiplier is one. No implicit spin factor or two-dimensional height conversion is introduced. Gaussian broadening uses `exp(-(x/epsilon)^2)/(sqrt(pi)*epsilon)`.

Response symmetry reduction, Projector/Wilson/Geometric responses, Full metric/Christoffel F completion, nonorthogonal JC/THG and arbitrary source-gauge conversion remain unavailable. No SCF, NAO generation, pseudopotential construction or real-material benchmark is implied by these finite-model interfaces.

For admitted Conventional Integral charge/finite-model orbital tasks, explicit `ExecutionOptions(fourier_backend="mixed", NKdiv=..., NKFFT=...)` evaluates the native source operators through the existing bounded Fourier block store. Full FFT is the same provider with `NKdiv` equal to one on every axis and `NKFFT` equal to the mesh; `"full_fft"` is a scientific report label, not a new public backend spelling. Focused provider and public SC/IC/SHG Direct-parity pilots have passed on their own frozen source identities. They are engineering evidence. ## Explicit OpenMX boundary

```julia
input = WannierNLQG.IO.read_openmx_localized_input(path;
    format_version="scfout_v3", byte_order=:little)
```

Admitted protocols are declared `"3.8"` (six-integer header) or `"scfout_v3"` (seven-integer header, encoded binary version three). Software release identity is not guessed from the header. Version-three position moment orders 1..8 and both declared byte orders are bounded; only first moments are admitted into r. Legacy 3.8 remains scalar-only. Version three adds explicit flag-1 collinear and flag-3 spinor parsing; callers must supply `magnetic_state` and `spin_orbit_coupling` for spinful files. Flag 3 stores four real H blocks and three imaginary H blocks; imaginary up/down mixing is retained when SOC is off. The down/up block at R is the adjoint of up/down at -R. Spinful ordering is up then down, each with the same persisted atom-major spatial orbital order and centers. S/r are repeated spin identities. Metadata records the raw spin flag, declared magnetic/SOC state and multiplicity one. Model/file memory budgets are explicit and include raw/effective copies and spinor expansion.

Version three defaults to original binary Cartesian Gxyz (Bohr), including unwrapped molecular coordinates. Raw first position moments are relative to the bra atom; its Cartesian origin times S is added. Version 3.8 defaults to appended final fractional coordinates, retaining that declared historical interface; `position_source=:binary_cartesian` is also available. Replacing negative binary molecular coordinates with wrapped printed fractional coordinates changes the position operator and must not be inferred silently.

`unit_convention=:legacy_openmx` explicitly uses 27.211399 eV/Hartree and 0.529177249 Angstrom/Bohr. Chemical potential is recorded in Hartree; E_temp is retained as an uninterpreted upstream record, with no guessed unit. The full default import retains raw H/S/r and explicitly constructs the effective paired operators. An explicitly requested `operator_scope=:hamiltonian_overlap` reads the complete file, records its raw position translation defect, and admits only H/S. This scope cannot qualify positions or responses. Input SHA, units, coordinates, ordering, admitted operators and thresholds participate in the native model digest.

OpenMX position moments are grid-integrated quantities; equality at the current project admission thresholds is not guaranteed by the file format. Historical strict input-scope failures remain failures and are not evidence of file corruption or unusable DFT. Original WS2/CH4 raw inputs and independent diagnostics are retained outside the package payload. Historical raw position or metric failures remain failures, independent of prior MPI rank agreement or an upstream importer that overwrites partners.
